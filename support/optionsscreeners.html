Options Screeners — Support

Options Screeners finds option trades that fit your rules: cash-secured puts, covered calls, LEAPS, earnings plays, 0DTE, credit and debit spreads, collars, risk reversals, iron condors and poor man's covered calls.

Getting started

Every screener works right away in Demo mode, using made-up sample prices. To screen with live data, open Settings, pick tastytrade (or MarketData.app) as the data source, and enter your own API credentials. Settings explains where to find them. No tastytrade account yet? You can open one here (this is the developer's referral link; the developer may receive a bonus, at no cost to you). Your first 5 live-data screens are free; after that, live data needs an Options Screeners Pro subscription.

Subscription

Options Screeners Pro is $1.99 per month and renews automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app if you reinstall or switch phones.
