WifeSaidSo — Support

WifeSaidSo is a shared reminders app for couples and households. It keeps track of the things you were asked to do, including the ones that come back: take out the recycling every week, change the furnace filter three months after you last did it.

Sharing with your partner

Open the group button at the top left, choose Manage Groups, create a group such as Family, then tap Share This Group and send the invite in Messages. When your partner taps the link, the group appears on their phone. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud. Sharing a group needs WifeSaidSo Pro; people you invite join free.

Reminders for one person

In a shared group, set For to one person. Only their phone alerts, and the reminder is view-only for everyone else, so you can see when it's done. Each reminder shows who added it, and its history shows who completed it.

Groups

Make as many groups as you like, such as Family, Kids' Activities or Gym Friends, and share each with different people. Swipe left on a group you created to delete it; this deletes it for everyone it's shared with. To leave a group someone else shared, open it and tap Manage or Leave.

Alerts on shared reminders

Everyone's phone alerts for shared reminders. Keep WifeSaidSo running in the background (don't swipe it away in the app switcher) so changes from other phones arrive promptly; a phone where the app was swiped away catches up the next time it's opened.

Repeating reminders

A reminder can repeat on a schedule (every Saturday, the 3rd of every month) or after you complete it (3 days after each time you water the plants). Completion-based repeats count from the day you actually finish, so a late task doesn't pile up.

Snooze vs. move

Snooze only delays the alert; the due date stays the same. Move changes the due date itself. Swipe left on a reminder to use either.

Alerts

Allow notifications when asked so alerts can fire. Reminders with a date but no time alert at your default alert time, which you can change in Settings.

Subscription

The free version includes 3 repeating reminders and unlimited one-time reminders. WifeSaidSo Pro unlocks unlimited repeating reminders and sharing groups for $1.99 per month or $14.99 per year, renewing automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app if you reinstall or switch phones.
