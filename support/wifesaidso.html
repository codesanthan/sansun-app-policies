WifeSaidSo — Support

WifeSaidSo keeps track of the things you were asked to do, including the ones that come back: take out the recycling every week, change the furnace filter three months after you last did it.

Repeating reminders

A reminder can repeat on a schedule (every Saturday, the 3rd of every month) or after you complete it (3 days after each time you water the plants). Completion-based repeats count from the day you actually finish, so a late task doesn't pile up.

Snooze vs. move

Snooze only delays the alert; the due date stays the same. Move changes the due date itself. Swipe left on a reminder to use either.

Alerts

Allow notifications when asked so alerts can fire. Reminders with a date but no time alert at your default alert time, which you can change in Settings.

Subscription

The free version includes 3 repeating reminders and unlimited one-time reminders. WifeSaidSo Pro unlocks unlimited repeating reminders for $1.99 per month or $14.99 per year, renewing automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app if you reinstall or switch phones.
