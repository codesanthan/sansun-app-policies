Sharpest

Sharpest is an iPhone app that helps you clean up your photo library honestly. Everything is analysed on your iPhone.

What it does

  • Finds duplicate photos and keeps the best copy.
  • Groups similar shots and suggests the sharpest one to keep.
  • Sorts screenshots into pictures, documents and slides, stacks screenshots of the same document or deck, and saves a stack as one PDF.
  • Lists your largest videos, and can archive them to your own Google Drive with a verified copy before you choose to remove them from Photos.
  • Optionally finds photos of passports, licenses, ID and payment cards so you can hide or delete them.
  • Adds a date, time and place stamp to photos (reversible in Photos).

Google Drive archive

Sharpest uploads the videos you choose straight from your iPhone to a "Sharpest Video Archive" folder in your own Google Drive. It can only access files it creates there. Videos stay in Photos until each copy is verified and you choose to remove them. See the privacy policy for details.

Common questions

I deleted photos but my storage didn't change. Deleted items go to Photos › Recently Deleted for 30 days. Space frees up when you empty it.

A video says it's only in iCloud. With iCloud "Optimize iPhone Storage", originals live in iCloud. Sharpest downloads the original first, uploads it, then deletes the temporary copy.

How do I disconnect Google Drive? In Large Videos › Google Drive archive, tap Disconnect. Your Drive files stay in your Drive.
