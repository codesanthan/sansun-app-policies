CounterCard

CounterCard shows your name, phone number and other details you choose in big, clear text, so you can turn your phone around at the pharmacy, doctor's front desk or any counter. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Getting started

  • Tap Create your card (or +) and enter a label, your name and phone number.
  • Choose which details are visible under Visible on your card.
  • Tap Show card. Tap ✕ (Hide) when you're done.

Family cards

  • Create a card for each family member. While a card is showing, swipe left or right to switch.
  • Tap ↑↓ on the home screen to change the order.
  • To share a card with a family member who has CounterCard, open the card's ⋯ menu and choose Share all details or Share name & phone only. They open the file and tap Add card. Sharing again updates their copy.

Insurance

  • Edit a card and tap Scan insurance card or Choose a photo of the card. Scan the back too for the pharmacy RxBIN, RxPCN and Rx Group numbers. Always check the filled-in numbers against your card.
  • Insurance shows only when you tap Insurance on a shown card.
  • Some places still need to see your physical insurance card or ID.

Common questions

How do I show my date of birth or address? Turn them on in the card's editor, or long-press or triple-tap a shown card to reveal them; do it again to hide them.

How do I add the Lock Screen widget? Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, tap the widget area and add CounterCard. Touch and hold the widget to choose which card it opens.

Can my family use my purchase? Yes. CounterCard is a one-time purchase that works with Apple Family Sharing: family members in your Family Sharing group can download it from the App Store at no extra cost.

Are my details uploaded anywhere? No. CounterCard has no account and no servers. See the privacy policy.
