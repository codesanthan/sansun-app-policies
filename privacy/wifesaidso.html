WifeSaidSo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 (sharing and groups)

WifeSaidSo is a shared reminders app for couples and households, with repeating reminders, snooze and postpone. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data.

No account with us

WifeSaidSo has no account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information.

Where your reminders are stored

Your reminders, groups, notes, due dates, repeat rules and history are stored on your iPhone and synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account; we have no access to it and run no servers of our own. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your iPhone.

Sharing with people you invite

If you share a group, the people you invite can see and change that group's reminders and history, including the name shown on the things you add or complete. That name is your Apple ID first name unless you set a different one in the app. Invitations, membership and syncing are handled by Apple's iCloud sharing. Groups you don't share are visible only to you. You can stop sharing a group, or leave one you joined, at any time.

Notifications

Reminder alerts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to WifeSaidSo Pro, your subscription is managed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

WifeSaidSo does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not use advertising identifiers.

Data deletion

Delete a reminder in the app, then remove it from Settings → Recently Deleted. Deleting a group you own deletes its reminders, including for anyone you shared it with. To remove the app's iCloud data, go to the Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, since it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the app's App Store page.
