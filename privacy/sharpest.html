Sharpest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Sharpest is an iPhone app that helps you clean up your photo library: duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, large videos, photos of ID documents and cards, and an optional archive of large videos to your own Google Drive. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data.

No account with us, no servers

Sharpest has no Sharpest account and runs no servers of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, photos, videos or any other personal information, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Your photo library stays on your iPhone

With your permission, Sharpest reads your photo library to find duplicates, pick the sharpest shot, sort screenshots and, if you choose, look for photos of IDs and payment cards. All of this analysis runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. Results are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting or hiding photos uses Apple's Photos permissions and confirmation; deleted items go to Photos' Recently Deleted.

Date stamp

If you add a date and place stamp to a photo with location turned on, Sharpest asks Apple's map service to turn the photo's location into a place name. That request goes to Apple, not to us, under Apple's privacy policy.

Google Drive video archive (optional)

If you choose to archive videos to Google Drive, you sign in with Google on Google's own sign-in page; Sharpest never sees your Google password. Sharpest asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it create and access only the files and folders it creates in your Drive (a "Sharpest Video Archive" folder). It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

  • What is sent, and where: the original video files you select are uploaded directly from your iPhone to your own Google Drive. They do not pass through any Sharpest server. Google stores them under your Google account and its terms.
  • What Sharpest reads from Google: your Google account email (to show which account is connected), your Drive storage quota (to check space), and the size and checksum of the files it uploaded (to verify each copy before you choose to remove the original from Photos).
  • Where your Google sign-in is kept: the access tokens are stored in your iPhone's Keychain. Upload progress records are stored on your iPhone.
  • Disconnecting: tap Disconnect in the app to sign out and revoke Sharpest's access. Your Drive files stay in your Drive until you delete them. You can also remove Sharpest's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharpest's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the archive feature you request. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone else, and not read by people.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Sharpest Pro, your subscription is managed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your iPhone.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

Sharpest does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not use advertising identifiers.

Data deletion

Sharpest's on-device results are removed when you delete the app. Archived videos are in your Google Drive, where you can delete them. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children's privacy

Sharpest does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, since it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the app's App Store page, or via the Sharpest support page.
