Options Screeners — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Options Screeners screens stock options (cash-secured puts, covered calls, spreads, LEAPS and more) on your iPhone. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data.

No account with us

Options Screeners has no account or sign-in with us. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information.

Your brokerage and data-provider credentials

If you choose to screen with live data, you enter your own tastytrade API credentials (client secret and refresh token) and, optionally, a MarketData.app API key. These are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. The app uses them to connect directly from your device to tastytrade and MarketData.app to read quotes, option chains, your watchlist and your positions. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The App Store version of the app cannot place trades.

What the app stores

Your screener settings and a count of the free live-data screens you have used are stored locally on your device. Screening results are not saved or sent anywhere.

Demo mode

Demo mode uses sample data generated on your device. It makes no network requests.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Options Screeners Pro, your subscription is managed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

Options Screeners does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not use advertising identifiers.

Third-party services

tastytrade and MarketData.app are independent services, not operated by us. Their own privacy policies govern the data they hold about you. Options Screeners is not affiliated with either.

Not investment advice

Options Screeners is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Options involve risk and are not suitable for all investors.

Data deletion

Use "Clear Credentials" in the app's Settings to remove your saved credentials, or delete the app. We hold no copy of your data on any server, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, since it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the app's App Store page.
