CounterCard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

CounterCard is an iPhone app that shows your name, phone number and other details you choose in large text, so you can show them at a pharmacy, front desk or counter. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data.

No account, no servers, nothing collected

CounterCard has no account and runs no servers. We do not collect, receive or store your name, phone number, date of birth, address, insurance details or any other information. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Your cards stay on your iPhone

The cards you create are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its Lock Screen widget. Device backups (iCloud or computer) may include your saved cards, under Apple's terms. Deleting a card, or deleting the app, removes the saved details from the app.

Lock Screen widget

The widget shows only the label you gave a card (for example "Me" or "Mom"), never your details. Tapping it opens the card after your iPhone is unlocked.

Scanning an insurance card

If you scan an insurance card or choose a photo of one, CounterCard reads the text on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition to fill in the plan, member ID, group and pharmacy (Rx) numbers. The photo is not saved and is never uploaded. The camera is used only while you are scanning.

Sharing a card

If you share a card, CounterCard creates a small file and hands it to the method you choose (AirDrop, Messages, Mail or another app). It goes only to the person you send it to; it does not pass through us. You can choose to share only the name and phone number, keeping date of birth, address and insurance on your iPhone.

Purchase

CounterCard is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

CounterCard does not include any analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not use advertising identifiers.

Children's privacy

CounterCard does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the app's App Store page, or via the CounterCard support page.
